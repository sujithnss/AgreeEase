AgreeEase helps stamp paper vendors prepare rental/license agreements: a customer sends details over WhatsApp, our system extracts the relevant information, our staff review it, and a draft and final agreement document are produced.

Information we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp to request an agreement, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Names, addresses, and other details you provide for the agreement (e.g. tenant/landlord names, property address, rent, deposit, and agreement duration)
  • The content of your messages to us

We do not collect Aadhaar numbers or other government ID numbers through WhatsApp.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to prepare the rental/license agreement you requested, to communicate with you about that request on WhatsApp, and to keep an internal record of the agreements we have drafted for our own business and legal record-keeping.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc., as the provider of the WhatsApp Business Platform we use to receive and send messages
  • Our AI service provider, used to automatically extract agreement details from the text of your messages
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf

Data retention

We retain agreement requests and related records for as long as needed for our business, legal, and tax record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion, subject to our legal obligation to retain records of agreements we have drafted.
